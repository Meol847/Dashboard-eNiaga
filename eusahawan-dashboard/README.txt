eNiaga@create Business Performance Portal - XAMPP

1. Copy this folder into C:\xampp\htdocs\eusahawan-dashboard
2. Start Apache and MySQL in XAMPP.
3. Import database/eusahawan.sql into phpMyAdmin if the database has not already been created.
4. Open http://localhost/eusahawan-dashboard/

The My Business page now has three separate tabs:
- Sales Reports: sales filters, KPIs, charts, transactions and CRUD.
- Business Profile: editable dummy business profile saved in browser localStorage.
- Monthly Performance: yearly monthly sales comparison, KPIs, chart and 12-month table. It reads directly from the MySQL sales table.

Changing, adding or deleting sales updates Monthly Performance when that tab is opened/refreshed.
